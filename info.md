## OWASP Sri Ramakrishna Engineering College

OWASP Sri Ramakrishna Engineering College (OWASP SREC) is a student-led cybersecurity chapter dedicated to building a strong culture of application security, cybersecurity research, and technical learning within the student community.

The chapter provides a platform for students to learn, collaborate, and gain practical experience through cybersecurity workshops, technical sessions, capture-the-flag competitions, seminars, security research, and community-driven initiatives.

OWASP SREC focuses on areas including web application security, ethical hacking, penetration testing, secure development, vulnerability research, cloud security, and emerging cybersecurity technologies.

Through collaborations with cybersecurity professionals, industry communities, and other student organizations, the chapter aims to connect students with real-world security practices and encourage students to contribute to the broader cybersecurity community.

### Our Objectives

- Promote cybersecurity and application security awareness among students.
- Provide hands-on learning opportunities through workshops, CTFs, and technical sessions.
- Encourage security research, vulnerability discovery, and responsible disclosure.
- Help students develop practical skills in offensive and defensive security.
- Facilitate collaboration between students, industry professionals, and security communities.
- Encourage participation in open-source security projects and the wider OWASP community.

OWASP SREC is committed to creating a collaborative environment where students can explore cybersecurity, develop practical skills, conduct research, and contribute to a safer digital ecosystem.
